> [!abstract] About This Note
> A **threat actor** is any individual or group that poses a security risk to systems, networks, or data. Their motivations, skill levels, and methods vary widely — from nation-state APTs to lone script kiddies.

---

# Types of Threat Actors

## Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs)

> [!danger] High-Severity Actors
> APTs are highly skilled, well-funded attackers who infiltrate a target and remain **undetected for extended periods**. Often state-sponsored.

**Characteristics:** Highly organized, long-term operations, targets governments and critical infrastructure

**Motivations:** Intellectual property theft, cyber espionage, infrastructure disruption

---

## Insider Threats

> [!warning] Already Inside the Perimeter
> Individuals with **authorized access** who intentionally or accidentally misuse that access. Especially dangerous because standard perimeter defenses don't apply.

**Who they are:** Employees, contractors, vendors, business partners

**Motivations:** Sabotage, corruption, espionage, data theft, data leaks

---

## Hacktivists

Threat actors motivated by political, ideological, or social causes rather than financial gain.

**Goals:** Protest, spread propaganda, promote social change, gain publicity

# Hacker Categories

| Category | Also Known As | Intent | Method |
|----------|--------------|--------|--------|
| **Authorized** | White Hat | Improve security | Pen testing with permission |
| **Semi-Authorized** | Security Researcher | Discover flaws | Report responsibly, no exploitation |
| **Unauthorized** | Black Hat | Personal gain | Exploit systems without permission |

# Exam Tips

> [!tip] Key Distinctions
> - **APT** → advanced, stealthy, long-term — often nation-state
> - **Insider Threat** → has legitimate access; that's what makes them dangerous
> - **Hacktivist** → politically or socially motivated, not financial
> - **White Hat** → ethical, works *with* permission
> - **Black Hat** → malicious, works *without* permission
> - **Script Kiddie** → low skill, uses existing tools
